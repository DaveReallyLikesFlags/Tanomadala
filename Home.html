    • Tanomadala, formally the Socialist Democratic Micronation of Tanomadala is a micronation situated in South Jakarta, Indonesia. Our nation was formed on the 19th of April, 2026; however, prior to the existance of a Tanomadalan state, there existed one now called the Ruandami Republic. Even prior to the Ruandami Republic, our nation has had its own history in micronationalism since mid 2023-2024.
    We are officially a one-party unitary parliamentary republic under the shared power of the Tanomadalan Socialist Party and Council of Foreign Diplomats. This system of government was mainly inspired by that of the government structures of the United Kingdom, Federal Republic of Germany, and the People's Republic of China. Even though we are mainly based in Jakarta, we do own territories outside of the region and even outside of Indonesia entirley, with one of them being situated inside another micronation.
    We firmly believe in the ideals of nationalism and democratic-socialism. We strive to create a perfect socialist-democracy for our people and make sure that their needs are met and their rights are preserved and protected at all costs, as we believe that the people are what matter most in building a nation (micronational or not) just like ours.
