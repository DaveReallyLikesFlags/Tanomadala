    Tanomadala, formally the Socialist Democratic Micronation of Tanomadala is a micronation and direct sucsessor state to the Ruandami Republic, situated in South Jakarta, Indonesia, with various exclaves scattered across Southeast Asia. We do not seek full independence from Indonesia; however, we do take our role as a micronation very seriously. We believe in the ideals of democratic-socialism and nationalism. Although we are a micronation, we strive to achieve a perfect society following said principles and encourage the world to follow along, as in a nation (micronationalist or not), the number one priority must be its people.
