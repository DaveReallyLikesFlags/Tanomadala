    • Big Update to the Tanomadalan Legislature - Presidential Annoucement


    29/09/2026 - 6:50 (UTC+7)

    Good morning, good afternoon, good evening, or goodnight, dear comrades.
    Hello everyone! Sorry for being inactive recently... I've just been a bit more busy with my personal life. Anyhow, I've been thinking a lot about how I can improve this micronation, and I've come to a conclusion. After discussing it with the parliament (kinda), we've decided to reform the way our legislative branch functions entirely!

    • We will be abandoning our one-party system and allowing other parties to form once again! Under one condition! All parties formed must follow a form of socialist ideology. Democratic socialism, socialism of the 21st century, Marxism – anything really.
    • The Tanomadalan Socialist Party (PST) will also now be renamed to the Great People's Party (Partai Rakyat Raya, PRR) to not claim the Tanomadalan name... If that makes sense.
    • Our legislature, now named the Representatives of the Red (Majelis Merah, MM), will host parliamentary elections at the end of every month to decide which parties get represented in the legislature. The chancellery elections will, however, still happen once a year on the 5th of October.
    • The Council of Foreign Diplomats (DDA) will be completely dissolved in favour of this new party system. Any foreign diplomat who wishes to participate in parliament must be a part of a party; any non-foreign diplomat may enter as an independent individual.

    These changes will take place on the 30th of September, 2026, UTC+7:00. We are still currently working on transitioning. If you wanna start your own party, join one, join the parliament, or just view it as a citizen, consider joining the nation's official Discord server! Thank you for taking your time to read this article. And always remember to
    stay tuned and stay proud, comrades!


    President Dave of Tanomadala
